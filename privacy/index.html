Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short

Lumiboard is made by Dillon Pinto. It has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your boards and photos stay on your device. This policy explains the few cases where information leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The boards you make, the photos you add, your settings and your reminders are stored only in the app on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting a board, or the app, removes them.

Your photos

You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, so the app receives only the photos you pick. Saving a finished board asks for permission to add pictures to your library, and nothing else.

Photo library search

If you use the built-in photo search, the words you search for are sent to Pixabay GmbH, which provides the photos, and the photos you choose are downloaded from it. Pixabay receives your internet address, as any website does. Your boards are not sent. Pixabay's privacy policy is at pixabay.com/service/privacy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. To confirm what you have bought and to restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase records (what was bought, when, and whether it renewed or was refunded) and basic technical information such as your country, device type and app version. It does not receive your name, email address, photos or boards. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics kits, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal information.

Reports from Apple

Apple may give us totals, such as how many people installed the app or how often it stopped working, if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers. These reports do not identify you.

Children

Lumiboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no account or profile for you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you write to us, we will have your email address and will use it only to reply. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, including for the anonymous purchase identifier held by RevenueCat, email us.

Where information is handled

Pixabay is based in Germany and RevenueCat in the United States. Information sent to them is handled under their own policies and the safeguards they describe.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
