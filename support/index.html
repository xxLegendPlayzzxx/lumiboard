Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I make a board?

Tap New board, choose a shape and a template, then tap any space to add a photo. Use Photos to fill several spaces at once.

Where do the photos come from?

From your own photo library, or from the built-in search of free photos, which are provided by Pixabay.

How do I crop or move a photo?

Tap the photo, then Crop. Drag to move it and pinch to zoom. On the board, drag with one finger, and pinch or twist with two fingers to resize and turn.

How do I use my board as a wallpaper?

Tap Save, then Save to Photos. Open the picture in the Photos app, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the apps wobble, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Lumiboard. There is one widget for your board and one for the daily affirmation. To choose the board it shows, open the board, tap Save, then Show on Home Screen. Widgets are part of Lumiboard Pro.

What is in Lumiboard Pro?

Widgets, unlimited boards, every template, font, sticker, background and affirmation pack, print-quality export and unlimited photos from the built-in library. Your first board is free to make, finish and save.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Lumiboard and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Where are my boards stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your boards. Save finished boards to Photos to keep a copy.
